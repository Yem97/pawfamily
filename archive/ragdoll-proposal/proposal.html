Good to know

Frequently asked questions

When can a kitten go home?

From 13 weeks old. By then they've had both vaccinations, been microchipped and vet checked, and are confident, litter-trained and fully weaned.

How does reserving work?

Once we've spoken and agreed a kitten is a good match, a deposit reserves them. The balance is due on collection. The deposit amount and terms will be set out here.

What comes with my kitten?

Pedigree and registration papers, vaccination record, microchip details, a starter pack of food and litter, a familiar-smelling blanket, and free pet insurance for the first weeks where available.

Are the parents health tested?

Yes. All our breeding cats are DNA tested negative for HCM (the Ragdoll variant) and PKD. Copies of the certificates are available on request.

Should a Ragdoll live indoors?

We recommend an indoor life, or a secure garden enclosure. Ragdolls are trusting and laid-back, which makes roads and strangers a real risk.

Do you deliver kittens?

We prefer families to collect so we can meet you, but we can discuss options if you live far away.
